Atelier · Our hand

We don't stock
a catalogue.
We keep a diary.

Wallantq began as a notebook of small obsessions — a corner of light, a finish of a floor, a particular green of an old door in Jodhpur. Every piece you see here was found, commissioned, or made because it answered something in that notebook.

There is no wholesale. No algorithm. If a piece speaks to you, write to us — and we'll tell you its story.

— the founder

Nº 012 · one of one

The Rajnigandha
Console.

A slow, heavy piece hand-turned from a single block of Jaisalmer marble. Named for the night-blooming tuberose that grows in the quarry town where it was made. Carries light unusually well.

Material
Jaisalmer marble
Dimensions
160 × 42 × 88 cm
Weight
124 kg
Finish
Honed, wax-sealed
Offered by private enquiry ₹ on request
Maker
Rameshwar Studio, Jaisalmer
Lead time
Ready to ship
Delivery
White-glove, complimentary
Signed and documented. Arrives with a hand-written card, care instructions, and the quarry's certificate of stone origin. Pieces may vary subtly due to natural veining.
Provenance

How this piece
came to be.

  1. 01
    Quarried in Jaisalmer

    A single block of marble selected from a working quarry outside the old city, chosen for its density and the honey-gold cast it holds at dusk.

  2. 02
    Turned by hand

    Rameshwar, third-generation stonecutter, worked the piece over eleven days on a floor lathe his grandfather built. No CNC, no templates.

  3. 03
    Honed, not polished

    A honed finish is softer underhand and ages with use — a patina rather than a shine. Sealed with a natural bee-wax blend.

  4. 04
    Signed and shipped

    Crated in-studio, with a hand-written card naming Rameshwar, his town, and the week the stone was cut.
